abstract: >-
      This paper proposes a hierarchical spatial transformer for massive point samples, addressing challenges like long-range dependencies, irregular distributions, and high computational costs. Using quad-tree-based multi-resolution learning, efficient spatial attention, and uncertainty quantification, the model outperforms baselines and scales to one million points on a single NVIDIA A100 GPU.
